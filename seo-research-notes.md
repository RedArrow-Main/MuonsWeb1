# SEO Research Notes

## Official guidance applied

Google’s SEO Starter Guide emphasizes making content easy for search engines to crawl, index, and understand; descriptive URLs, logical site organization, canonicalization, useful content, and internal/external discovery are relevant to Muons Technology. It also notes that search impact can take weeks or months and that there is no guaranteed ranking formula.

Google’s structured-data guidance recommends JSON-LD as a maintainable format, requires the markup to describe visible page content accurately, and recommends validation with the Rich Results Test. The Muons site should therefore use Organization and other eligible schemas only where the corresponding facts are visible and supportable.

Google’s sitemap guidance says sitemaps help search engines crawl important pages and rich media, especially for new sites, larger sites, or sites with relatively few external links. The current site is a single-page landing site, so the sitemap should remain intentionally small until useful, unique content pages are created.

Google’s people-first content guidance warns against mass-produced search-engine-first pages and scaled content abuse. Programmatic SEO for Muons should therefore use a limited set of high-value, expert-reviewed pages with unique evidence, not thin location or keyword doorway pages.
