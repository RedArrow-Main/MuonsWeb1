# Fieldwise — Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### 1. Field Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** A grounded, editorial system inspired by field notebooks, satellite maps, and seasonal journals. It pairs deep agricultural greens with a vivid chartreuse signal for a confident, infrastructure-minded brand.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Glasshouse Atlas
**Very Brief Intro:** A light, architectural approach inspired by greenhouse framing, agronomic diagrams, and daylight. It presents technology as precise, clean, and quietly optimistic.

**Probability:** 0.03

### 3. Night Irrigation
**Very Brief Intro:** A dark, atmospheric direction built around evening field operations, water systems, and low-light sensing. It feels technical, watchful, and premium without becoming cyberpunk.

**Probability:** 0.09

---

## Chosen approach: Field Ledger

### Design Movement
**Contemporary agrarian editorialism** with visual cues from field guides, cartographic marks, and operational dashboards. The reference site’s direct, mission-led storytelling and high-contrast hero are translated into a distinct Fieldwise identity rather than replicated.

### Core Principles
1. **Nature is operational:** Crop intelligence is represented as a system of active signals, not decoration.
2. **Editorial confidence:** Large, sharply composed type and direct copy build authority without corporate stiffness.
3. **Asymmetric storytelling:** Content moves in deliberate columns, overlapping panels, and staggered cards rather than predictable centered grids.
4. **Signal over noise:** Chartreuse is used sparingly to identify action, data, and momentum against deep ink-green surfaces.

### Color Philosophy
The base palette begins with **Ink Soil** — a near-black green that gives photography and data the weight of critical infrastructure. It is balanced with **Field Linen**, a sun-warmed off-white that recalls crop labels and hand-held field books. **Signal Sprout** is the unmistakable accent: an almost-electric yellow-green used only where the interface asks the visitor to notice, decide, or move. The palette feels rooted, international, and high-functioning instead of rustic.

### Layout Paradigm
The page is designed as a **vertical field report**. A dark, image-led opening establishes the mission. From there, each section uses a different reading rhythm: full-bleed statement, split technical dossier, horizontal signal cards, and a wide closing contact panel. Sections do not simply stack cards in equal columns; they create visual pauses and directional movement.

### Signature Elements
1. **Contour-line overlays** referencing topographic maps and field boundaries.
2. **Signal pills and data ticks** in chartreuse to mark active intelligence.
3. **Offset image windows** with narrow border captions, suggesting an annotated field ledger.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions are understated and purposeful. Navigation scrolls smoothly to the relevant report section. Primary buttons move forward with a short directional arrow shift; cards lift subtly and reveal a signal line on hover. There is no decorative motion without a structural role.

### Animation
On load, the hero eyebrow, headline, and actions enter in a short staggered sequence using opacity and a modest upward translate. Contour lines drift almost imperceptibly under the hero imagery. Cards use a 180–220ms cubic-bezier ease-out hover state, while buttons compress gently on active press. All non-essential animation respects reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
**DM Serif Display** carries headings in a high-contrast, editorial voice. **Manrope** serves as the durable interface and body typeface, with uppercase tracking for labels and navigation. Headlines are large, left-weighted, and broken deliberately across lines. Body copy remains open and readable, never overly condensed.

### Brand Essence
**Fieldwise turns ground-level signals into confident agricultural decisions for producers, cooperatives, and institutions.**

Personality: **rooted, precise, forward-looking.**

### Brand Voice
The voice is clear, field-aware, and quietly urgent. Headlines speak in productive verbs and concrete outcomes; CTAs invite a practical next step rather than a generic conversion.

Example headline: “Make every growing season more knowable.”

Example CTA: “See the field signals.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The Fieldwise mark is a modular **F** constructed from a field parcel, a sprouting leaf, and a single offset signal dot. The wordmark uses a custom-feeling, spaced Manrope treatment paired with the mark. The icon can stand alone in the header and as the favicon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Signal Sprout — #C8FF2B.**

## Style Decisions

- The Fieldwise icon is given clear, primary placement as a framed modular mark in the navigation and footer so its parcel, sprout, and signal-dot construction reads at a glance.
- Signal Sprout is reserved for actions, priority indicators, active-status marks, and decisive outcomes. Large decorative chartreuse fills are avoided.
- Every major image panel functions as an **annotated field record**, using concise parcel labels, seasonal records, map-corner marks, and operational status notes to connect imagery to decision-making.
- Reference-informed additions use clear evidence chips, a dark operating-principles ribbon, field-signal lenses, and a four-step season cycle. These ideas strengthen the Field Ledger narrative without adopting the reference’s proprietary claims, metrics, or product language.
- The About Muons section is treated as the company-level chapter behind Fieldwise: a dark editorial dossier with three mission cards, a focused technical-leadership record, and a direct Delaware location card.
- U.S. identity is carried through the company’s Delaware grounding, “America’s working lands” positioning, and an American Field Record motif rather than broad patriotic decoration. The Regenerative Agriculture chapter uses soil, water, diversity, and seasonal learning as practical, non-certification stewardship themes.
- Signal Sprout is limited to action surfaces, active labels, status dots, small rule accents, and key emphasis. The Fieldwise modular mark is treated as a recurring report stamp in navigation, footer, and the stewardship log. Lower page chapters use offset cards and season-note labels to sustain the compiled-field-report rhythm.
